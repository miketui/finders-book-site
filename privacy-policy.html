How we handle your data

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 August 2026

Short version: we collect an email address if you give us one, we use standard tools to run a checkout and see whether the page works, and we do not sell anything about you to anyone. The long version is below, because "trust us" is not a privacy policy.

What we never see: nothing you write inside The Finder's Book. The PDFs are files on your device. They do not sync, phone home, or transmit anything. We have no access to your entries, and there is no server where they could be stored.

Who we are

Joanne Godfrey and Michael David Warren Jr., publishers of The Finder's Book — The Family Clarity System™. Contact: info@familyfindersbook.com. We are the data controller for the information described here.

What we collect, and why

DataWhenWhyLegal basis
Email address, optional first name When you request the Family Readiness Gap Check or subscribe To send the checklist and occasional notes about the book Consent
Order details — email, purchase amount, product, country for tax At checkout To deliver your files, provide support, and meet tax obligations Contract & legal obligation

Support correspondence When you email us To answer you and keep a record of the issue Legitimate interest

We do not ask for, want, or store: passwords, PINs, recovery codes, full account numbers, Social Security numbers, or medical records. If you email any of those to us by accident, we will delete the message and tell you we did.

Payment information

We never see your card number. Checkout is handled by Payhip and its payment processors. Your card details go directly to them and are never transmitted to or stored by us. We receive only the order confirmation: what was bought, for how much, and the email to deliver it to.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information. We never have.
  • We do not share your email with other companies or publishers.
  • We do not build advertising profiles about individuals.
  • We do not send email to people who did not ask for it.

Cookies and similar technologies

The site uses a small number of cookies and browser-storage entries:

  • Your analytics preference is stored in your browser so we can remember whether you allowed or declined optional analytics.
  • Session storage holds campaign parameters (such as utm_source) for the length of your visit, so we can tell which channel sent you. It is cleared when you close the tab.
  • Vercel Web Analytics measures aggregate page views and named site events without setting analytics cookies by default. It is not loaded until you allow analytics.
  • Google Analytics 4 is not loaded until you allow analytics. Once allowed, it sets first-party cookies (such as _ga and _ga_*) to distinguish visitors and measure conversions. These cookies expire after up to 2 years. Google’s privacy terms are at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • No advertising pixel is currently active on this site.

Use the Privacy choices control at the bottom of any page to allow, decline, or withdraw analytics consent. Withdrawing stops future site events and removes Finder’s Book GA cookies that are accessible to this site. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings. The site works without optional analytics; only the measurement stops.

How long we keep things

  • Email subscribers: until you unsubscribe, then removed within 30 days.
  • Order records: as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically 7 years.
  • Analytics: in aggregate, per the provider's retention settings.
  • Support email: up to 24 months, then deleted.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to:

  • Tell you what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct anything inaccurate
  • Delete it
  • Send you a copy in a portable format
  • Stop using it for a particular purpose
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Email info@familyfindersbook.com. We respond within 30 days and we do not charge for this.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what we collect, to delete it, to correct it, and to opt out of sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA, so there is no "Do Not Sell" mechanism to operate — there is nothing to opt out of. We will never discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.

UK and EU residents (UK GDPR / GDPR)

You have the rights listed above, plus the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority — the ICO in the UK, or your national data protection authority in the EU. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting processing already carried out.

Email you receive from us

Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link in the footer. It works immediately. Transactional messages — your receipt and your download link — are sent because you bought something, and are not marketing.

International transfers

Our processors may store data in the United States and elsewhere. Where data leaves the UK or EEA, transfers rely on the safeguards our processors maintain, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

Children

This site and product are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us their details, email us and we will delete them.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Payment data never touches our systems. Access to the email list is limited to the two of us. No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise — but we have deliberately designed this so that the sensitive material stays on your device rather than ours.

Changes

If we change this policy we update the date at the top. Material changes affecting how we use your information will be emailed to subscribers before they take effect.
